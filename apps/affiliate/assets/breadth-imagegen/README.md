# Breadth draft image sources

These five PNG files are AI-generated, unbranded editorial scene bases for the unpublished `breadth-2026-08-next` cohort. They are not photographs of the exact products or proof of hands-on testing.

`scripts/generate-breadth-draft-images.mjs` converts each site base into product-family-specific WebP artwork. Every output identifies the family, its simpler comparison option, and its exact verified ASIN. The public images therefore function as decision worksheets, not merchant product photography.

Generation mode: `photorealistic-natural`.

- `network.png`: realistic rack, patching, fiber/copper test gear and backup-power workbench; cool slate palette and left-side negative space.
- `smarthome.png`: lived-in home corner with generic local controls, lighting, climate, garden and kitchen devices; warm daylight and left-side negative space.
- `homeoffice.png`: practical apartment desk with generic scanner, label printer, privacy display, audio/video and cable-management equipment; neutral daylight and left-side negative space.
- `baby.png`: safety-conscious nursery and feeding-prep corner with generic feeding, storage, wipes, stroller and bath equipment; no child and no unsafe sleep setup.
- `pet.png`: humane pet-care and travel-prep entryway with generic stairs, carrier, trailer, harness, leash, water and cleaning equipment; no animal or medical claim.

All prompts prohibited readable text, logos, watermarks and branded products. Release QA must still confirm that the illustration cannot be mistaken for the exact checkout item.
