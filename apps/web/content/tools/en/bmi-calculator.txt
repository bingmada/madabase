A BMI calculator estimates body mass index from height and weight. It is a quick reference for understanding whether a weight sits in a common underweight, normal, overweight, or obesity range. [FAQ]
What does BMI mean?
BMI means body mass index. It compares weight with height and gives a simple population-level reference number.
Is BMI a medical diagnosis?
No. BMI does not measure body fat, muscle, age, or health history. Use it as a rough reference, not a diagnosis.
Can athletes have a high BMI?
Yes. People with more muscle can have a higher BMI even when their body composition is healthy.
