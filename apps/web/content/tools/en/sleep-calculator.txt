[FAQ]
Why use 90-minute cycles?
Many sleep planning tools use 90 minutes as a rough sleep-cycle estimate. Real sleep cycles vary by person and night.
Does this guarantee better sleep?
No. It is only a planning aid. Light, caffeine, stress, routine, and health factors also matter.
Should I include time to fall asleep?
Yes. If you usually need 10 to 20 minutes to fall asleep, account for that before choosing a bedtime.
