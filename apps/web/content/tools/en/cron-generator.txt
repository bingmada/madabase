[FAQ]
What is a cron expression?
A cron expression is a schedule string. The common Unix format has five fields: minute, hour, day of month, month, and day of week.
What does star mean in cron?
The star symbol means every allowed value for that field. For example, a star in the month field means every month.
How do I run a cron job every 5 minutes?
Use */5 * * * * in the common five-field cron format.
How do I run a cron job every day at 9 AM?
Use 0 9 * * *. This means minute 0, hour 9, every day, every month, every weekday.
Is this also a cron parser?
Yes. The page helps you read the meaning of common cron expressions by showing the generated expression and upcoming run times.
Do all systems use the same cron syntax?
No. Some systems support seconds, years, names, or special symbols. Always check the syntax supported by your scheduler.
