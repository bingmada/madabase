[INTRO]
A calorie calculator estimates daily energy needs from simple inputs such as age, sex, height, weight, and activity level. The result is a planning estimate, not a personalized nutrition prescription. It can help you compare maintenance, weight-loss, or weight-gain scenarios before deciding what to track more carefully.

[FAQ]
Is this calorie estimate exact?
No. It is an estimate based on common formulas and simple assumptions.
Can I use it for weight loss planning?
You can use it as a starting reference, but health goals should be adjusted carefully and ideally with professional advice.
What affects daily calorie needs?
Age, sex, height, weight, body composition, activity, sleep, stress, and health conditions can all affect the number.
