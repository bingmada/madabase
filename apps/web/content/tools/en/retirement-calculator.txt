[FAQ]
Is this retirement calculator exact?
No. It is a simplified projection for planning and comparison.
Should I include inflation?
If available, compare both nominal and inflation-adjusted assumptions. Inflation can materially change long-term purchasing power.
Can returns be guaranteed?
No. Investment returns are uncertain, so it is useful to compare conservative and optimistic scenarios.
